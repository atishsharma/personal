Daily 70-Jar Broth (5 Liters)

Yields enough for 5 Autoclave runs (70 Jars). 70 x 70ml = 4.9L. 5L prevents wastage while allowing a 100ml buffer.

  • RO Water 5 Liters
  • Dextrose / Sugar 100g
  • Peptone 50g
  • Yeast Extract 50g
  • MgSO4 7.5g
  • KH2PO4 5g

Master Agar (500ml)

  • RO Water 500ml
  • Malt/Dextrose 10g
  • Agar Powder 10g

1. Mix: Combine ingredients in a 1L Erlenmeyer flask. Drop in magnetic stir bar.

2. Heat: Place on hot plate stirrer. Heat and stir until clear (no clumps).

3. Sterilize: Cap with foil. Autoclave at 121°C (15 PSI) for 45 mins.

4. Pour: Let cool to ~45°C. In front of HEPA Hood, pour into sterile petri dishes.

Production LC (1 Liter)

  • RO Water 1,000ml
  • Dextrose 20g
  • Peptone 10g
  • Yeast Extract 10g

1. Mix: Combine ingredients in a 2L flask. Drop in magnetic stir bar.

2. Dissolve: Stir on hot plate (low/no heat) until perfectly dissolved.

3. Sterilize: Foil cover. Autoclave at 121°C (15 PSI) for 45 mins.

4. Inoculate: Room temp, move to Flow Hood. Inject 10ml purchased LC OR drop 2 Master Agar wedges. Incubate on stirrer.
